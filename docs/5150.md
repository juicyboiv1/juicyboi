# 5150 model (Rev2.4): Rhythm + Lead, Post Gain power section, 4x12 load

Rev2.4 replaces the Rev2.3 "5150-inspired Lead" engine. The goal (Cory): match
**Red v2's thickness and compression, not more**; fix the even-heavy, thin
Lead; add the Rhythm channel with Bright and Crunch; and lay out the faceplate
like the real amp (no Master). Code: `Source/dsp/Amp5150.h`; per-amp settings:
`docs/per-amp-state.md`. No commercial branding appears in the UI.

Sources: the amp's schematic (sheets 1–3, EVH 120 preamp drawing 81501510,
already cited in `docs/5150-presence.md` / `docs/5150-resonance.md`), the
owner's manual (front-panel description of Bright, Crunch, Pre/Post Gain,
Resonance, Presence), and forum traces of the channel relays. Cory's photo was
private to an earlier subagent and was not read.

## Signal flow

```
shared input calibration (input_trim; not a faceplate knob)
 -> 5150 boost (OD-808 / SD-1, its own settings)            } 4x oversampled
 -> Rhythm cascade  or  Lead cascade (both run; 30 ms crossfade on RHY/LEAD)
      V1A -> Pre Gain network -> V1B -> V2A (cold) -> V2B -> V5B -> V5A
 -> x Post Gain of that channel (two-decade audio taper; 0 = silent)
 -> shared passive TMB (Low / Mid / High)
 -> Power5150 (non-mirrored RecoveryStage: FX-return/PI/power-tube stand-in)
 -> 5150 post EQ -> Presence -> Resonance (one NFB loop)     } base rate
 -> SpeakerLoad5150: 4x12 impedance peak against the closed-loop output
    impedance of that same loop -> 5150 cab -> 5150 gate (dry-keyed)
```

The Post knob drives the power section, so it sets compression as on the
real amp. There is no Master on this faceplate.

**Preamp Out.** The global `preamp_out` toggle (drawer front, next to Input
Cal) skips the 5150 SpeakerLoad, Presence, Resonance and cab, and keeps the
cascades, TMB, Post Gain, Power5150, post EQ and gate. See
`docs/per-amp-state.md`.

### Pre Gain networks (real values, nodal MNA solve)

| | Lead | Rhythm, Crunch off | Rhythm, Crunch on |
|---|---|---|---|
| series | R29 470K ∥ C17 .0022µ | R43 2.2M ∥ C13 470p | R19 470K ∥ C51 270p |
| shunt at pot top | R41 1M | R21 1M + (R23 39K + C63 .1µ) | R21 1M (39K branch lifted) |
| pot | VR2 1M audio | VA1 1M audio | same |
| top→wiper | R40 1M ∥ C19 .001µ (bright) | C27 180p (+ C10 470p with Bright) | same |

The source is V1A's plate (R2 220K ∥ rp ≈ 48K), coupled by C6 .022µ. A 1K
carbon-track end resistance keeps Pre 0 very quiet rather than dead.
`Amp5150Test` checks the time-domain network against the analytic nodal
magnitude: worst error 0.026 dB.

### Stage corners

The low-pass corners are the real Miller/RC corners (V1A 13 kHz, V1B 2.3 kHz,
V2A 30 kHz, V2B 2.1 kHz + 4.4 kHz, V5B 22.7 kHz, V5A 2.2 kHz). The coupling
high-passes (V1A 25, V1B 45, V2A 55, V2B 70, V5B 65, V5A 25 Hz) stand in for
cathode-bypass shelves and grid blocking. They are **tuned**, not sheet values:
they set the 80–110 Hz weight and the 40 Hz difference tone to Red v2's.

### Tuning (Voicing5150 defaults)

preGainTrim 3, V1A drive 1.4. Biases: V2A .32, V5B −.15, V5A .25 (drive 4.5).
Output LP 12 kHz. Power: postScale 17, drive 2, bias −0.40, pos 1.3, neg 1.0,
out 0.33. Speaker: Re 6.4, Res 35, f0 100 Hz, Q 3, zOpen 24 Ω. rhythmTrim 3.5,
rhythmCrunchTrim 0.6.

## Rhythm channel: interpretation and uncertainty

- **Channel relays.** The schematic's channel relay K1 (K1A, K1B) switches the
  V1B cathode (C26 22 µF extra bypass, Rhythm) and the V2A grid divider (R20
  220K shunt, Rhythm). Both tubes are shared by the two channels.
- **Crunch.** The front-panel Crunch switch drives relay K4. From the sheet
  and forum traces, K4 swaps the Rhythm series network (R43 2.2M ∥ C13 470p →
  R19 470K ∥ C51 270p). It also lifts the R23 39K + C63 .1µ shunt and the R20
  loss at V2A, so Crunch gives more gain and a tighter low end.
  *Uncertain:* the K4 contact wiring (which pole lifts which shunt), and
  whether C51 is 220p or 270p (both readings appear; 270p is used).
- **Bright.** The manual describes Bright as "+6 dB at 2 kHz". It is modeled
  as C10 470p from the pot top to the wiper, added to C27 180p.
  *Uncertain:* whether the S1 (Bright) switch connects C10 across the pot or
  to another node. The sheet scan is ambiguous at that switch. With the
  sheet's 470p, the model gives +9.3 dB at Pre 3, +7.9 at Pre 5, +5.1 at Pre 7
  and 0 at Pre 10. The manual's +6 dB falls near Pre 6.5. The part value was
  not changed to force the manual's figure.
- **S1/S2.** We take S1 to be Bright and S2 to be Crunch. This is our reading
  of the front-panel switch designators, not a verified trace. S2 drives K4 rather than switching audio directly; the
  coil wiring is not modeled. Switching morphs the parts over about 30 ms, so
  it is click-free (tested).
- **Level calibration.** rhythmTrim 3.5 makes the Rhythm channel clean to
  edge-of-breakup at Pre 3–5 (THD −28 to −42 dB, mostly H2) and crunchy from
  Pre 7. With Crunch on (×0.6), it stays below the Lead channel's gain at the
  same Pre and converges at 10. Both are judgement calls, not measurements of
  a real amp.

## Limitations

- Static nonlinearities: no grid conduction or bias-shift dynamics, and no
  sag. The tuned high-passes stand in for these effects.
- At the hottest setting (gain 10, 0.25 input), the Lead goes odd (e−o +1.3 dB
  vs Red's +8.1). At low input it is a little more even than Red.
- Above 3 kHz, the 5150 is darker than Red v2: −3.3 dB at 6 kHz re 1 kHz, vs
  Red's −0.5. Presence/High compensate.
- Palm-mute gap-flub at gain 8 is −17.4 dB (Red v2: −19.6). That is slightly
  more bloom.
- Forward gain A = 20 in the NFB loop and the 4x12 parameters are nominal,
  not measured.
- Rev2.4 renders differ from Rev2.3 for every 5150 setting, by design. Only
  the Rectifier is bit-identical.
